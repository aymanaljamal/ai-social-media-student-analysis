# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in this project.

Everyone who contributes to, reports issues in, reviews, or otherwise participates in this project is expected to behave respectfully.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and professional.
* Giving constructive feedback.
* Being open to different approaches and ideas.
* Focusing on the project and technical discussion.
* Helping others learn and improve.
* Accepting constructive criticism.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks.
* Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Abusive or offensive language.
* Any behavior that makes the project environment unsafe or hostile.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in restrictions on participation in the project.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

---

## Contact

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the appropriate private communication channel available to the project maintainers.
